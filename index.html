JANUARY 1950 · A POLITICAL SIMULATION

WORLD LEADER

The Cold War is just beginning. Korea is about to explode, empires are crumbling, and the bomb is no longer America's alone. Pick one of 34 countries and lead it as its head of government, under its real 1950 system, through 76 years of history that may not happen the way you remember.

🏛️ Real governmentsPresidential, parliamentary, semi-presidential, monarchy, one-party, junta, collegial, colony. Each has its own way to lose power: impeachment, no-confidence, coups, purges, palace plots.
📜 Whip the votesDraft bills and count votes faction by faction. Whip, trade favors, hand out pork, or skip the legislature and rule by decree.
🏭 Build a nation14 industries, literacy, technology and industrialization. Grow from a rice-paddy economy into a superpower.
🌍 A living worldEvery other country acts on its own. History happens on schedule, until you change it.
👤 Your characterBackstory, traits, skills, ideology, party and a face. Then pick your cabinet.
